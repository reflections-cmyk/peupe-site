Nonnie Karanja, Founder

Hi, I'm Nonnie.

I've built my career stepping into organizations during their messiest moments: contracts being consolidated, the same vendors paid from different accounts, and no single picture of the spend. My work has been building the reports, trackers and frameworks that turn that into clear decisions.

My journey started in Kenya, in small businesses and in public service with the Kenya Revenue Authority and the Kenya Police Service. Over ten years, I've worked across the public and private sectors in both Kenya and the United States, bringing a cybersecurity lens to procurement and operations.

Now I bring that clarity to small businesses that are ready to grow. I named the business Peupe, the Kiswahili word for stainless, because that's what I want for every business I work with.
